Our Heritage & Craft · Maitama, Abuja

Architects of Joy, Baking One Sweet Memory at a Time

Mimi's love for baking began at a young age, spending hours in the kitchen learning the secrets of creating balanced, tender cakes. After years of perfecting her craft, Mimi opened her own bakery in Abuja to share her creations with the community.

We believe that quality ingredients and meticulous attention to detail are essential for creating truly exceptional treats. Sharing sweet treats is a way to connect with loved ones, celebrate special occasions, and create lasting memories.

Our Mission · The Treats By Mimi Promise

Why We Bake

At Treats by Mimi, we believe that life's sweetest moments deserve a touch of magic. We are not just bakers; we are architects of joy.

01. Milestones

Celebrate Life’s Milestones

From birthday candles flickering on frosted masterpieces to the shared joy of a warm croissant on a Sunday morning, we make every occasion sweeter.

02. Community

Nurture a Community of Kindness

Our bakery at 28 Usuma Street is more than a counter and display case — it is a haven for warm connections and the simple pleasure of sharing with loved ones.

03. Craft

Inspire Through Creativity

We constantly refine our flavor architecture and buttercream textures to bring you innovative delights that awaken your taste buds.

04. Integrity

Bake With a Conscience

We use the finest locally sourced Nigerian ingredients, treated with care and respect so every bite reflects our commitment to quality.
